Nogrammerly

A writing assistant for your Mac. It checks spelling, grammar, clarity and tone in Slack, Notes, Mail, Zoom, Sublime Text and most other apps, and can rewrite sentences for you. Everything runs on this Mac. Your text is never sent anywhere.

Install

  1. Drag Nogrammerly onto the Applications folder in this window. If you are asked, choose Replace.
  2. Eject this disk (drag it to the Bin, or press the eject button in Finder's sidebar).
  3. Open Nogrammerly from your Applications folder. Do not open the copy on this disk.
  4. If macOS says the app "cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it", open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Set up

The Setup window opens on first launch and shows which steps are done. You can reopen it any time from the ✦ menu-bar icon or Help › Nogrammerly Setup Guide.

  1. Allow Accessibility access. Click Open Accessibility Settings, then turn on Nogrammerly in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. Nogrammerly needs this to read and correct the text field you are typing in.
  2. Everywhere mode starts automatically once access is allowed.
  3. Optional: turn on Apple Intelligence (System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri) for AI grammar, clarity and tone checks and for sentence rewrites. Without it you still get spelling, grammar and style checks.

Use

  • Type in any app. Problems are underlined: red = correctness, blue = clarity, purple = delivery, orange = style.
  • A round button sits in the bottom-right corner of the field you're typing in. Green ✦ means no issues; red shows how many suggestions there are. Click it to see every suggestion, or to rewrite your selection or the whole text in a Professional, Friendly, Confident or Concise tone, even when there are no mistakes.
  • Right-click an underline to see the suggestion and choose Replace, Copy suggestion, Rewrite sentence (Professional, Friendly, Confident or Concise), Ignore or Add to dictionary.
  • Replacements can be undone with ⌘Z in most apps.
  • The ✦ icon in the menu bar shows how many issues the current field has. Click it to see every suggestion, rewrite the whole text, pause everywhere mode, or open the Setup Guide.
  • The Nogrammerly window has its own editor with a score, tone and a ✦ Rewrite button.

Troubleshooting

  • Accessibility is switched on but the Setup window still shows it amber. macOS is holding an approval for a different build of Nogrammerly. In the Setup window click Reset & Ask Again, then switch Nogrammerly on once more in System Settings. (Builds from version 1.1 onward are signed with a stable identity, so this should only be needed once.)
  • Underlines don't show in one app. Some apps don't tell macOS where their text sits on screen. Use the ✦ menu-bar icon instead; it still lists and applies every suggestion.
  • Grammarly or another checker is installed. Their underlines may overlap. Turn one of them off to avoid duplicates.
  • AI suggestions are missing. Check that Apple Intelligence is turned on and has finished downloading. The first AI check after launch can take a few seconds.

Uninstall

Quit Nogrammerly from the ✦ menu, drag it from Applications to the Bin, and remove it from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.
